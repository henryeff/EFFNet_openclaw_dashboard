# Landing Offer A/B Planner (Prototype)

A lightweight local prototype to estimate conversion-test upside before writing code.

## What it does
- Inputs: monthly traffic, current conversion rate, average lead value, expected uplift
- Outputs:
  - projected monthly revenue upside
  - extra leads/month
  - prioritized experiment backlog (impact/effort)

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in your browser.

## Why this matters
Helps pick high-ROI conversion experiments quickly, reducing low-impact testing work.
